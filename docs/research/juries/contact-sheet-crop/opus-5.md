🗳️ **Juror 1** (Claude Opus 5, 1M context) — **VOTE: C — refuse `--crop` composed with a range, permanently for this effort**

**Reasoning:** The feature's entire justification is that a sheet makes a claim of coverage an agent can act on. The founding experiment's load-bearing failure was a sampler that was structurally blind and whose silence read as coverage. A caller-typed band reintroduces exactly that failure with the tool's own signature on it: the `photo-07` defect is not merely harder to see on a cropped sheet, it is annihilated — and that defect is the one the research says *only exists as a relation between tiles*, i.e. the one capability the sheet uniquely has. Shipping an option whose effect is to delete the sheet's unique power, while leaving it looking clean and complete, is shipping the bug the effort was founded to prevent.

Disclosure cannot rescue it, and fact 3 is the proof measured on this exact flag: an accurate, printed, correct disclosure (`half scale, 492x170…`) was read past because the documentation promised otherwise. Disclosure has now been empirically shown to be a weaker defence than the effort's ratified principle assumes, and the one place we have data is the crop flag itself. A defence that has already failed once on the same surface is not a defence I will spend the sheet's integrity on. Worse, the mechanism is different in kind from `blind_to`: blindness of the rule is constant and learnable, so a fixed enumeration eventually gets internalised. A caller-typed rect makes the blind spot *variable per call and authored by the reader*, which is the configuration in which humans and agents most reliably forget what they excluded. Fact 4 confirms this doesn't fit the taxonomy — needing a third disclosure category to make one flag safe is the design telling you the flag is foreign to the instrument.

And the value it buys is already available. Fact 2 is decisive: single-instant `--crop` is now lossless and true-scale today, so the two-step loop — whole-frame sheet to locate the instant, cropped single frame to read the fine detail — delivers the fine-detail legibility with zero new specification and zero coverage claim at risk. The only thing per-tile crop adds over that loop is *scanning* fine detail across many instants without knowing where to look, and the single class that needs it (`word-08`, a text defect) has non-pixel channels per fact 6, while the class with no other channel at all (`sentence-08`) was never even tested on a cropped sheet (fact 1). So the measured "highest-value mode" rests on one hand-eyeballed rectangle, one confirmed win, one confirmed annihilation, and one untested case. That is not an evidence base that earns a permanent hole in the instrument's central claim.

**Trade-offs:** This costs the real 2.33× linear scale, and it makes fine-detail-across-many-instants a two-call loop with a human-or-agent guess about which instant to zoom. That is a genuine loss of ergonomics, and I accept it because a slower honest instrument beats a fast one that can report "clean" over a region it never rendered.

Not B: B is the intellectually honest repair, and I would rather have B than A — but it is unbuilt, unmeasured, and by fact 5 the document has no region vocabulary, so a derived band inherits a refusal surface (rotation, non-unit scale, RTL) that would make the highest-value mode refuse unpredictably on ordinary documents. Worse, "union of the rects of every element that changed" is only safe against defects that live in a *changed* element's rect; `photo-07`'s wrongness is in a photo whose rect may not be in the union at that instant, and a colour-collapse defect is a relation between an element and what's *behind* it, so the guarantee "cannot exclude a changed element" is narrower than the guarantee the sheet needs. B ships a construction-level promise it cannot actually keep. If anyone wants B, it must be built and measured against all three planted defects first — which means it isn't this effort's decision.

Not A: A is B without even the construction-level promise, defended only by the one mechanism that has already been measured failing here.

Fact 7 is satisfied: this is an explicit, permanent refusal with a defined answer — `--crop` with `--from/--to` errors, and the error names the two-step loop as the supported path. The error message is where the teaching goes, not a caveat buried in a sheet.
