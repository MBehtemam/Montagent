# Court packet: the audio map's measured check, round 2

Ticket: [What measured check must every audio capability's ADR name, and what runs it?](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/802),
on the audio map ([#795](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/795)). The packet is
blind: it carries context and options, and no recommendation.

## Context

Montagent is a video-editing format: a JSON project document that an agent writes, `validate`
checks from the file alone, and `render` turns into an MP4 by driving the ffmpeg the user
installed (floor: ffmpeg 7.1). Audio is one mix graph per project
(`atrim → atempo → aloop → [audio_effects list] → volume → adelay → amix normalize=0`, then an
optional master stage of one measured gain and `alimiter`; 48 kHz stereo, AAC 160k). An effort is
adding audio effects in CapCut's and Premiere's image: loudness normalisation, limiter, EQ,
compressor, gate, noise reduction, de-ess, crossfades, reverb/echo, pitch, voice changers. Each
gets its own ADR. The lead workflow is TTS narration over a music bed. An `audio_effects` member
can be bypassed with `"enabled": false`. Restoration (noise reduction, de-ess) is inert on clean
TTS and waits for a recorded-voice fixture.

Facts:

- **Determinism** (measured): every candidate filter is byte-identical within one ffmpeg build,
  across runs and thread counts. Across builds and CPU SIMD paths, `firequalizer`,
  `superequalizer`, `afftdn`, dynamic `loudnorm` and the pitch chain change bytes.
- **CI** runs `cargo test` on Linux (pinned ffmpeg 7.1 floor build), macOS (Homebrew latest) and
  Windows (Chocolatey latest).
- **AAC 160k** adds inter-sample peaks after a correctly applied limiter, and trims some energy
  near the top of the band.
- **The ADR evidence rule** (repo): a numeric claim in an ADR needs a committed re-executable
  check, committed with its input data. A qualitative claim needs the artifact itself committed
  verbatim. Before an ADR is marked accepted, its cited evidence must exist and run clean.
- **ADR-0172** promises that a document without `master` renders byte-identically to before.
- **Borrowed thresholds** are cited and make a finding `review`, not `error` (ADR-0061).

**Settled in round 1** (the owner ruled; do not reopen):

1. Every capability ADR names a repo integration test that renders a fixed fixture through the
   real pipeline on every CI leg. It is a port of the prototype's evidence script (same metric,
   same fixture), asserted at a stated tolerance, never by byte hash. `verify` (which measures
   the user's deliverable independently of the engine) checks only what `master` declares.
2. Measurement uses ffmpeg's own meters (`ebur128=peak=true`, `astats`, a filter plus `astats`
   or `aspectralstats`). The ADR names the meter, and the test pins its parse. A Rust FFT is
   allowed for band energy only if the ffmpeg-filtered measurement proves noisy across legs.
3. `verify` tolerances: loudness ±1 LU both directions; true peak a `review` above
   `ceiling_dbtp` + 0.5 dB, **provisional until a committed script measures AAC 160k overshoot
   after `alimiter`**.
4. A prototype's A/B: the clips the owner judged are committed as a record, the listen is blind
   X/Y with a committed key, and the verdict is in a file next to the clips that the ADR quotes.
5. A smoke check (reverb, voice changers): renders; length exact or within a stated bound;
   48 kHz stereo; not silent; no NaN/Inf or clipping; not a no-op against bypass; loudness
   within a generous window of bypass; echo and reverb also assert their tail. Pitch shift is
   measured (fundamental shift on a sine fixture), not smoke.

## Questions

Answer each one.

**Q6 — Fixtures: what does a capability's test render?**
(a) synthetic signals generated by the test with ffmpeg's lavfi sources (sines, sweeps,
impulses, seeded noise), nothing binary committed;
(b) committed real audio (voice, music);
(c) a split by purpose, for example synthetic for the numbers and one shared committed real
fixture for the ear's A/B;
(d) other. Say also where a fixture's licence and provenance are recorded.

**Q7 — Is the number measured on the PCM of the mix before encoding, or on the decoded AAC?**
(a) always the decoded AAC (the deliverable);
(b) always PCM before encode (the encoder swapped out of the same graph);
(c) by claim, for example PCM for capability tests and decoded AAC for delivery claims;
(d) other. Also: do the A/B clips the owner hears go through AAC?

**Q8 — What is the tolerance convention for a capability's check?**
Decide what each check must state and how its tolerance is justified. For example: whether the
expected value comes from the filter's definition, from bypass, or from a previous render;
whether checks are absolute or relative to bypass; and where a tolerance comes from (a cited
external figure, the measured spread across the three CI builds plus a margin, or other).

**Q9 — Does every capability also carry a bypass identity check?**
(a) yes: within one test run, a document with the member `enabled: false` produces PCM
byte-identical to the same document without the member, and a document using no new feature
produces the same mix graph as before the capability landed;
(b) yes, but by tolerance rather than bytes;
(c) no;
(d) other.

**Q10 — Who measures the provisional 0.5 dB true-peak allowance, and when?**
(a) within this ticket, before its ADR is accepted: a committed script on the floor build runs
a hot signal through gain, `alimiter` at −1 dBTP, AAC 160k and decode, measures the overshoot,
asserts it, and the allowance moves to the measured figure if it differs;
(b) a separate task ticket later, with the ADR stating 0.5 dB as provisional meanwhile;
(c) other. Say also what signal the measurement uses.

**Q11 — Where is all of this written down?**
(a) one ADR ("every audio capability names a measured check") holding the round-1 and round-2
rulings as a standing contract, amending ADR-0172/ADR-0117 for `verify`'s tolerances and fixing
the evidence folder layout, plus a pointer line in the repo's agent docs;
(b) amend ADR-0172 for `verify`'s tolerances, and put the contract in agent docs only, no ADR;
(c) split across several ADRs (one for `verify` tolerances, one for the test contract, one for
the A/B evidence);
(d) other.
