# Brief B: talking-head social cut

**10 seconds · 1080×1920 · 30 fps · 9:16.** Deliver one MP4 with sound.

A vertical social clip: the person who builds Montagent talks to camera for ten seconds. It
has to read with the sound off, so the captions carry the words. It has to feel like a brand's
own channel, not a raw phone video.

Everything you need is in the asset pack; its `README.md` lists it. The presenter is
`presenter/take-1.mp4`, filmed against a green screen; its words and their timings are in
`presenter/take-1.words.json`. The music is `music/bed-120bpm.wav`. The product clip is
`screen/session.mp4`.

## Beat by beat

| When | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–10 s | The presenter is keyed off the green screen and stands over a background in the brand's accent colour. |
| throughout | Captions show what the presenter is saying, and the word being spoken is highlighted as it is said. |
| 1 s | A lower-third name bar wipes on: **Mohammad Bagher Ehtemam**, with **Builds Montagent** beneath. |
| 6 s | The name bar wipes off. |
| mid-clip | A circular picture-in-picture of the product pops in on the right and stays until near the end. |
| throughout | The music sits under the voice, dips while the presenter speaks, and fades out at the end. |

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] No green is left around the presenter, including at the hair and shoulders, on any frame.
- [ ] The background behind the presenter is the brand accent colour.
- [ ] Each caption word is highlighted while it is being spoken, and not before or after.
- [ ] The captions never cover the presenter's face.
- [ ] The name bar wipes on at 1 s and off at 6 s; it is a wipe, not a fade.
- [ ] The picture-in-picture is a circle with the product visible inside it, and it pops in with a small overshoot.
- [ ] The voice is clear over the music; the music is noticeably lower while the presenter speaks.
- [ ] The music has faded to silence by 10 s.
