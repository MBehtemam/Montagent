This directory already holds the finished owl short from brief C. `hoot.montagent.json` is its

## The change

The client says the owl talks too fast. Slow the voice line to **85 % of its speed** without
changing its pitch, and keep everything in step with the slower words: the beak, the wave on
"Hi… Hoot", the card and the gesture on "…as a file", the picture change on "movie", and the
lockup and cheer after the line. The video may run longer to fit; nothing else should change.
