# Census: After Effects 3D, scored against Montagent

Research for [#902](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/902), part of the map
[#896](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/896).

**Date of research:** 2026-10-10. **AE version read:** After Effects 26.5 (the September 2026
release), the latest in Adobe's
[release notes](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/what-s-new/release-notes-after-effects.html).
Per Adobe's [What's new](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/what-s-new/whats-new.html)
page, 26.2 (April 2026) brought parametric meshes, Substance materials with displacement, and
shadow-casting Spot, Parallel and Environment lights, and 26.3 (June 2026) brought in-engine depth
of field in Advanced 3D. Animated environment lights, the 3D helper commands and model animation
controls in the Properties panel are older: their pages date from April 2025. Materials on 3D text
and shapes and layers as material sources are in After Effects (beta) as of September 2026.

**Sources.** Adobe's After Effects user guide on helpx.adobe.com, one page per row below. No
secondary write-up is cited.

**How the pages were read.** Verified against the live pages on 2026-10-10. Every Adobe link
below was fetched in full; links that redirected have been replaced by the canonical URL, and
every row's description was checked against its page. The area's table of contents in the live
user guide (Work with 3D compositions, 3D layers, Cameras and lights, Import 3D models, 3D Channel
effects, Immersive video and VR) was walked, and the features the first draft missed were added.
Where two Adobe pages still disagree, the row follows the newer page and says so (FBX is "beta"
only in the January 2025 FAQ; the extraction page lists OBJ but also says OBJ carries no camera
or light data). No row is left unverified.

**How Montagent was scored.** From this repository at `main` 51aa4b5 (the rows added in
verification were scored against the same tree): `CONTEXT.md` (Transform,
Projection, Layer, Origin), `docs/adr/` (ADR-0040, ADR-0145, ADR-0150, ADR-0155, ADR-0156,
ADR-0166, ADR-0167, ADR-0168, ADR-0171 above all), `crates/montagent-core/docs/format.md` and its
pages, `schema/montagent.schema.json`, and the skills, chiefly the capability map in
`skills/montagent/SKILL.md` and `skills/montagent-prerender`. A row says "gap" only after a grep
of all of those for the feature's words (camera, depth, light, shadow, material, glTF/GLB/FBX/OBJ,
extrude, bevel, 360, equirect, VR, EXR, tracker, stereo) found nothing that covers or refuses it.

**Statuses.** *covered*: Montagent writes it. *partial*: Montagent writes part of it. *reachable*:
a skill or a documented workaround gets the picture. *owned by #591*: the open nest / group
transform map owns it. *refused by ADR-NNNN*: a decision rules it out. *gap*: nothing covers or
refuses it.

The frame for every row is ADR-0167's thesis: **an element may be projected, never placed.**
Montagent has a per-element Projection (`swivel`, `tilt`, `perspective`), and nothing has a depth,
nothing is sorted by z, and there is no camera. ADR-0168 fixes the angle order (CSS's
`perspective(d) rotateX(tilt) rotateY(swivel)`), the presence rule and `R-PROJECTION-SOFT`.

## Gaps worth a decision ticket

Grouped by what a ticket would decide. Each gap is weighed against ADR-0145's entry test: a
precedent in CapCut or Premiere, or the four invariants plus an accepted prototype.

### 1. The refusals need a home in the router skill

ADR-0167 refuses a camera, depth sorting and corner pin, but the capability map's "Not by design"
list in `skills/montagent/SKILL.md` does not name them, and its "What it can't do yet" line still
reads "Parenting, groups and cameras", which reads as if a camera is coming. An agent briefed with
"a camera move through 3D layers" learns the answer only from the ADR. A ticket should split that
line: a 2D camera move as a group transform stays with #591; a 3D camera, depth sort, inter-layer
shadows and corner pin go to "Not by design", each with its workaround (bake per-element keys;
a second element for a shadow; Projection for a tilted plane). Verification added four more
camera-shaped rows the same line would cover: camera data import (RPF, Maya, Vanishing Point),
Default Camera Settings, effects with a Comp Camera attribute, and Control Light with Camera.
No new decision, only a skill edit.

### 2. 3D content as a pre-render recipe

Models (GLB/glTF/OBJ/FBX), embedded model animation, parametric meshes, extruded and bevelled
text, PBR and Substance materials (now on 3D text and shapes too, in beta), image-based lighting,
and Cinema 4D scenes are all reachable
only by `montagent-prerender`'s generic code-drawn route. The skill names SVG and Lottie routes
but says nothing about a 3D scene, so an agent must invent the toolchain (Blender, a headless
three.js) and its determinism (GPU drift, sample noise) each time. Two decisions:

- whether a 3D model enters as a source at all, or is refused the way ADR-0171 refused SVG and
  Lottie, so that "not by design" can say so;
- whether `montagent-prerender` gains a fourth route for a 3D scene (model, extruded text, IBL)
  with a reference recipe whose decoded hash is stable.

### 3. Lights and materials on projected elements

Parallel, spot and point lights, Material Options, environment and animated environment lights,
and shadow catchers have no answer: neither covered nor refused. Premiere's Basic 3D, the precedent ADR-0167 used, has no lights, so these
could enter only by the entry test unless a precedent is found. A ticket should either refuse lighting on the
grounds ADR-0167 already gives (a light needs a position in space, which no element has) or
scope a per-element shading term driven by `swivel`/`tilt`. Refusing is the smaller decision.

### 4. Lens: depth of field and bokeh

Depth of field is reachable only as a hand-keyed `blur` per element, uniform across a tilted
plane. Camera Lens Blur's iris shape and highlight bloom are a gap. A ticket could ask whether a
lens-blur `effects` member (iris shape, highlight threshold) enters on its own, which does not
need depth, and whether a blur graded across a projected plane is wanted.

### 5. Bending a plane

Curvature and Segments (a video wall, a curved screen) are a gap. Projection keeps a plane flat,
and only text bends (ADR-0161). A curved `image` or `video` would be a new geometry beside
Projection; Premiere has no precedent, so it would need the entry test.

### 6. Per-unit projection on text

A `units` block cannot carry `swivel` or `tilt`, so AE's per-character 3D flip-in (letters turning
in one after another) is not writable. ADR-0151's units lists could gain the two angles; the
element-level `perspective` and ADR-0167 §5's eye bound would have to extend to each unit.

### 7. Tracking

The 3D Camera Tracker has no counterpart, and neither has 2D tracking. Placing a graphic on a
surface in footage needs it. That belongs to a tracking census, not this one; it is named here so
the map does not lose it.

### 8. 360 and VR

Equirectangular editing, cubemap extraction, seam-aware effects and spherical metadata on output
are all gaps with no ADR. This is a scope question first: whether Montagent makes 360 video at
all. Premiere's VR video support is the precedent to check first (not read for this census); a
ticket should decide scope before any mechanism.

### 9. Multichannel 3D renders as sources

Added in verification. RLA, RPF, PIC/ZPIC, EI/EIZ and multichannel OpenEXR files carry depth,
IDs and normals beside RGBA, and EXtractoR and IDentifier read them; Montagent has no such source
and no auxiliary channel. The 3D Channel rows that need depth are refused by consequence, so the
open question is narrower: whether a multichannel EXR enters as a source at all, or whether the
pre-render skill should say to split each pass to its own lossless footage. Refusing, with that
workaround named, is the smaller decision.
