# Premiere gap: AI and automated features

Research for [#893](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/893), part of the map
[#882](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/882). Area slug: `ai`. Re-sourced for
[#911](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/911).

- **Date:** 2026-10-10.
- **Premiere side:** Adobe's own help pages on helpx.adobe.com only: the Premiere desktop user
  guide (Premiere 26.5, plus the 27.0 beta pages), [What's new][wn], the [release notes][rn]
  (26.0, January 2026, through 26.5.2, September 2026) and the [beta features][beta] page.
  **Every page cited is now read in full** from the census fetch of all 807 guide pages
  ([#883](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/883)). The first version of this file
  was written while helpx.adobe.com was blocked and rested on search excerpts. Each row now
  cites the exact page or pages its mechanism comes from, marked **(F)** for fetched. Where a
  detail from the first version is on no page, the row says "unconfirmed" and keeps it. Where
  Adobe pages disagree, the newer page wins and the row says so.
- **Scope of "AI or automated":** a feature in which Premiere analyses media content (picture,
  sound or speech) or generates media, whether by a trained model or by a classic
  computer-vision or signal algorithm. Besides this area's census pages, the whole corpus was
  searched for "AI", "Firefly", "Sensei", "generative", "automatic", "auto ", "detect",
  "analyze" and "media intelligence". Automation that only reads file metadata or applies a
  setting (auto-save, relink, VR or log auto-detection from metadata) belongs to other areas
  and is listed under *Checked and left out*.
- **Montagent side:** tag `v0.2.0` (`51aa4b5` = `origin/main`): `CONTEXT.md`,
  `schema/montagent.schema.json`, `docs/adr/`, the MCP tool surface in
  `crates/montagent/src/mcp.rs` and `mcp/dispatch.rs`, and every file under `skills/`.
- **How a status is read.** Montagent contains no model (`CONTEXT.md`, **Montagent**), so no AI
  feature can be `have` inside the binary. The map's vocabulary applies by mechanism
  ([ADR-0145](../../adr/0145-capcut-and-premiere-are-where-precedent-is-looked-for-first-and-a-capability-with-none-enters-by-the-entry-test.md)):
  - `have`: an equivalent mechanism ships in the v0.2.0 binary or format.
  - `have (skill)`: a bundled skill or skill script produces the same result as ordinary file
    contents.
  - `partial`: the format can hold the result (or part of the job ships), but the analysis or
    generation step is missing. The row says which part.
  - `missing`: nothing equivalent ships, and no ADR refuses it. Many generated or analysed
    results could still be *placed* as an ordinary `video`, `audio` or keyframe list once an
    outside tool made them. That alone does not lift a row out of `missing`. A mechanism an
    ADR only *defers* is `missing`, not `refused`.
  - `refused`: an ADR rules the mechanism out; the ADR is cited.

## Checked and left out

- **Object removal or addition by generative fill in video.** No Premiere desktop page
  describes it; Adobe documents Content-Aware Fill in [After Effects][ae-caf] only.
- **Premiere on iPhone only:** Beat Detection, Speed Curves, AI speech cleanup
  ([iPhone what's new][iphone]). Desktop is the map's subject. For the record, beat finding
  exists as `beat_grid.py` in `montagent-craft`.
- **Media intelligence in Media Encoder** ([page][ame-mi]): a Media Encoder feature.
- **Remix in Audition** ([page][remix-audition]): Audition's own Remix, reached through Edit in
  Audition. It belongs to the other-apps area (`workflow-collab`); Premiere's own Remix is a row.
- **Essential Sound Repair sliders (Reduce Noise, Reduce Rumble, DeHum, DeEss, Reduce Reverb)**
  ([Repair dialogue][repair] (F)): effects with sliders, not analysis. They belong to the audio
  area and [`docs/research/audio-effects/PRECEDENT.md`](../audio-effects/PRECEDENT.md).
- **HSL and Luminance mask tools** ([page][hslmask] (F)): a mask keyed from a sampled colour or
  brightness range, a deterministic key rather than content analysis. The colour area has the
  row.
- **Metadata-driven automation:** VR auto-detection ([page][vr-auto] (F)) reads VR properties
  from the file, and Auto Detect Log Video Color Space reads camera metadata. Both belong to
  the VR and colour areas.
- **Color mode's Heads-Up Displays** choose a scope for the control being dragged
  ([Default Adjust operation][adjust] (F)): an interface choice, not analysis of the picture
  beyond the scopes themselves.
- **Third-party AI plug-ins** listed on the [plug-ins page][plugins] (F) (PluralEyes audio sync,
  Mocha planar tracking, Beauty Box face smoothing, Mercalli stabilisation): not Premiere
  features.
- **"Cognitive services" in Productions** ([page][prod-cog] (F)): the page describes outside
  tagging services feeding Premiere panels, not a Premiere feature.
- **Generative credits, model-output feedback storage, and AI Models preferences**
  ([feedback][feedback] (F), [AI Models preferences][ai-prefs] (F)): billing, privacy terms and
  links to Adobe's guidelines, not features. The feedback menu is noted in the
  Generated-media record row.

## Notable gaps

1. **No transcription anywhere in the bundle.** ADR-0051 refuses one inside Montagent, but no
   skill runs one outside either. Text-Based Editing, filler, pause and speaker deletion,
   Paper Edit, bleep, translation and single-word captions all depend on a words file the user
   must already have, and the words convention has no speaker or language key.
2. **Transcript-driven cutting.** The format and `shift` can hold a transcript edit, but no
   script turns "delete these words" or "delete pauses over 400 ms" into source ranges and a
   ripple.
3. **Generative media has grown.** Premiere 26.5 generates video (with partner models and
   first/last-frame transitions), sound effects, music and soundscapes in the timeline, keeps
   each result's prompt and references on the clip, and can regenerate it. Montagent has no
   route to any generator and no record convention for a generated source beyond ADR-0156's
   recipe for pre-rendered footage.
4. **Subject detection, mattes and tracking.** Object Masking, both kinds of mask tracking and
   Auto Reframe produce keyframes Montagent can already hold, but nothing makes them. Smooth
   object masks are per-pixel mattes, which have no way in (ADR-0150 refuses an element's
   matte, ADR-0152 defers an image-sourced mask), and Mask Expansion is refused by ADR-0152.
5. **Audio analysis and separation.** Enhance Speech, Separate Crosstalk, audio category
   tagging and sound search have no equivalent. The conventional `reduce_noise` and noise gate
   are still `proposed` and absent from the v0.2.0 schema.
6. **Colour analysis.** Auto Color and Color Match have only four global colour filters to
   write into; Color mode's analysed zones and hue patches need qualified adjustments that do
   not exist.
7. **Media search.** Media intelligence, sound search and Search Similar Frames have no index or
   cache; the agent looks at one contact sheet per clip.
8. **Interpolation and analysis effects.** Morph Cut, Optical Flow, Warp Stabilizer and audio
   sync are absent, and the pre-render skill has no recipe for outside tools that do them.
