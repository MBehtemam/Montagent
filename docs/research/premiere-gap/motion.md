# Premiere gap: motion, keyframing and compositing

Research ticket [#890](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/890), on the map
[#882](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/882) ("every Premiere Pro feature, set
against Montagent v0.2.0"). Re-sourced against the fetched pages by
[#911](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/911).

- **Date:** 2026-10-10 (first pass); re-sourced 2026-10-10 under #911.
- **Montagent side:** tag `v0.2.0` (`51aa4b5`, = `origin/main` when this was written).
  Read: `CONTEXT.md`, `schema/montagent.schema.json`, `crates/montagent-core/docs/format.md`,
  the ADRs named per row, the MCP tool surface (`crates/montagent/src/mcp*`: `compare`,
  `create_project`, `frame`, `measure`, `preview`, `query`, `render`, `shift`, `validate`,
  `verify`, `where`), and `skills/`.
- **Premiere side:** Adobe's own Premiere user guide on helpx.adobe.com, the current
  `/premiere/desktop/` guide (Premiere 26.5). **Source note (#911):** every row is now sourced
  to a page fetched whole by the census ([#883](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/883)),
  marked **(F)**. The first pass read search-engine extracts only, and some of its URLs were
  recalled locale or legacy `/premiere-pro/using/` addresses; each of those is replaced by the
  current guide page that says the same thing. This area's 36 census pages and the three
  What's-new pages were read in full. Pages from other areas are cited where a row's feature is
  documented there (Motion, speed, keying, Warp Stabilizer, Auto Reframe, nesting, Dynamic
  Link). A row whose mechanism no fetched page states is kept and marked **unconfirmed** in the
  URL column, never dropped.
- **Status vocabulary** (the map's): `have` · `have (skill)` · `partial` · `missing` ·
  `refused` (an ADR rules it out; cited).

53 rows were added and 27 rows corrected against the fetched pages. Four statuses moved from
`have` to `partial` because the page showed more mechanism than the first pass knew of: Clip-mode
mask adjustment, Ultra Key Spill Suppression, Ultra Key Color Correction, and Edge Feather.
The one new `refused` row is Object Mask's expansion slider, refused with Mask Expansion by
ADR-0152 §3.
Four rows are marked unconfirmed (Scale Width's Uniform Scale switch, a reverse segment inside a
Time Remapping curve, Picture-in-Picture presets, and the Transform effect's Skew).

Rows are one per mechanism: one per blend mode, per interpolation type, per mask control, per
Warp Stabilizer option and per keying effect. The 22 blend modes counted `refused` are
refused "for now" by ADR-0147, which needs a forcing case per added mode. They are not refused
on model grounds.

## Notable gaps

1. **No tracker anywhere.** Mask tracking (five directions, four combinable options),
   Warp Stabilizer and Auto Reframe all rest on frame-to-frame motion analysis, and no tool or
   skill does it. No ADR refuses it. Render-time analysis would break ADR-0146 criterion 2, so
   the natural shape is a tool or skill that writes ordinary keyframes (`x`/`y`/`scale`/
   `rotation`, mask rect or `points`), the way the ducking skill writes volume keyframes
   (ADR-0177).
2. **Premiere 26's masks scope effects; Montagent's cut alpha.** In 26.x every mask is attached
   to an effect (Opacity being one) or waits as an Unassigned mask. Montagent's `mask` only
   erases its element, so effect-scoped masks, unassigned masks, moving masks between effects,
   and mask union (Add) are all missing. Each needs a duplicate element today.
3. **A mask has no transform of its own.** Premiere 26 keeps a mask's Position, Scale,
   Rotation and Anchor apart from its path keys and adds them, which is what makes Clip-mode
   edits over a tracked mask work. Montagent moves a mask by `x`/`y` only, and a whole-clip
   change to a keyed mask means rewriting every key. That moved Clip-mode adjustment to
   `partial`.
4. **New mask sources:** HSL and Luminance mask tools, and Object Mask's Sharp/Smooth modes,
   Sharpness curve and offline-data handling, are all missing. A luma source is deferred by
   ADR-0152 §3. A colour-range source is named by no ADR.
5. **22 of 27 blend modes**, refused "for now" by ADR-0147 until each has a forcing case.
   The likeliest next ones are `darken`/`lighten`, `soft_light` and `difference`, which
   ADR-0147 names. Montagent also blends at 8 bits only.
6. **Anchor point is nine snap keywords.** There is no arbitrary pivot and no keyed pivot.
   Premiere's Anchor Point is free and keyframeable.
7. **Auto/Continuous Bezier and spatial interpolation.** Easing is per segment, so tangent
   continuity through a key is hand-computed and unchecked. A curved motion path has no
   spatial handles: `x` and `y` ease separately.
8. **Frame Blending and Optical Flow** are missing. Remapped or slowed video only samples the
   nearest earlier frame (ADR-0157 §3).
9. **Keying beyond chroma.** Luma Key and Ultra Key's Matte Generation extras, Matte Cleanup,
   three of four Spill Suppression controls, and hue correction are missing. Track Matte is
   refused (ADR-0150 §4). Image Matte Key and Difference Matte are missing too, but Premiere
   removed both in 26.0.
10. **Nesting with a group transform** is still undecided (ADR-0166; ruling on #632). A nest's
    local clock is refused (ADR-0001).
11. **Dynamic Link** has no live equivalent. The pre-render skill covers "render and replace"
    and Lottie-exported comps only.
