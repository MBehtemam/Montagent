under [#911](https://github.com/MBehtemam/Montagent/issues/911). Area slug: `captions`.

  (Premiere 26.5), every page read in full. No third-party source is used.
- **Montagent side:** tag `v0.2.0` (`51aa4b5`, = `origin/main`): `CONTEXT.md`,
  `schema/montagent.schema.json`, `docs/adr/`, the MCP tool surface in
  `crates/montagent/src/mcp.rs` and `mcp/`, and `skills/`.
- **Status vocabulary (map #882):** `have`, `have (skill)`, `partial`, `missing`, `refused`.
  Read by mechanism, not by name (ADR-0145).

**How the Premiere pages were read.** The first pass was written while helpx.adobe.com was
blocked and rested on search excerpts, with three rows marked *title-level*. For #911, the
census (#883) fetched all 807 guide pages. This file was re-checked against the 24 pages of
this area (the *Insert captions* subchapter and the *Text-Based Editing* chapter), the three
What's-new pages (What's new, release notes, beta), and a grep of the whole corpus for
captions, subtitles and transcripts. Pages from other areas cited below are *Export caption
tracks*, *Export selected captions*, *Export transcripts* and *Overview of export settings*
(export area); *Media Analysis & Transcription preferences*, *Media preferences*, *Graphics
preferences* and *Default keyboard shortcuts* (workflow area); the *Stylize text* pages on
track and linked styles (titles area); *Clean audio with bulk mute or bleep* (audio area);
*Check spelling using the Graphics tab*, *Media intelligence and Search panel*, and *Known and
fixed issues*. Every URL in the table is a fetched page, marked **(F)**. The URLs the first
pass cited outside `premiere/desktop/` (the old captions workflow, the translate-captions
FAQ, split-or-cut-clips, the 2024.1 What's-new page, the `mena_en` and `premiere-pro/using`
copies) are replaced by the current pages that carry the same facts.

**What the full pages changed.** 29 rows were added, and 15 rows had their Premiere mechanism
corrected. The corrections cover: Speech to Text (the dialog's audio-analysis choice; no page
says it is on-device or dates it to 22.2), the transcript correction round-trip (export and
re-import a .txt), Text-Based Editing (copy and paste rearranges clips, delete ripples), the
filler and pause filters (no minimum-pause setting is documented), bulk bleep (a censored-word
list, not a word selection), transcript export (formats now confirmed), open captions (the page
documents no legacy Open Captions type), CEA-608 (no page names channels CC1–CC4), caption
placement (Zone, Position, Size), the caption background (a style parameter, per-line or all
lines), Create captions and single-word captions (the dialog's settings are now read), caption
split (the fix is in 26.3, not 26.2), and caption merge (a release note confirms it exists). No existing row changed status.

## Montagent-only mechanisms (no Premiere page found)

Not rows, since the table lists Premiere features, but they bear on any later decision. The
full read of every caption, Text-Based Editing and What's-new page confirms that none of them
has a Premiere equivalent:

- **Per-word (karaoke) highlight** as a timed style window on a run (ADR-0048), with
  `E-HIGHLIGHT-RANGE`, `E-HIGHLIGHT-OVERLAP` (ADR-0051) and `R-HIGHLIGHT-UNPAINTED` (ADR-0134).
  Premiere's nearest is single-word captions, which show one word at a time rather than
  lighting one word inside a line.
- **Caption quality checks:** reading pace, repeat-duration disagreement, no audio backing,
  minimum duration (ADR-0034, ADR-0054, ADR-0071), and the `caption: false` opt-out (ADR-0136).
- **`N-CAPTION-SETTLES`** gives the readable instant of a staggered caption (ADR-0151).

## Notable gaps

1. **No caption delivery other than burn-in.** No sidecar (.srt, .scc, .mcc, .xml, .stl), no
   Text-panel caption export (.txt, .csv, .srt), no embedded 608/708/OP-47 stream, no MOV/MXF.
   Everything an .srt needs (text, `start`, `end`, the `caption` flag) is already in the
   document. Only the output is missing.
2. **No caption import.** No .srt or broadcast-format reader, no embedded-caption decode. A
   skill script would fit ADR-0051's "the agent converts outside Montagent" pattern.
3. **Caption formats (CEA-608, CEA-708, Teletext, EBU, OP-47) are absent.** A Montagent caption
   is pixels plus a flag. It has no stream semantics.
4. **Transcript-driven work is refused at the recognition step.** That covers Speech to Text,
   language packs and detection, speaker labels, speaker removal, filler words, the VAD model
   and translation (ADR-0051, ADR-0054). Text-Based Editing, Paper Edit, text and pause
   deletes, and bulk bleep are only partial: the edit primitives ship, but nothing links text
   to source time. No transcript is stored, so there is nothing to search, spell-check, correct
   or export.
5. **No shared styles.** No track style, redefine, usage indicators, linked or local style, or
   default caption font. Every value is a literal per element, repeated by a script.
6. **Text appearance gaps found on the full pages.** Only one stroke, always outside (no inner,
   centre or multiple strokes). There is no caption zone, no responsive position and no
   per-line background. Mask with Text is refused (ADR-0152).
7. **No language tag or track visibility**, so several caption languages can coexist but
   cannot be delivered one at a time.
8. **Timing against the audio on disk stays unchecked in core** (ADR-0051 and ADR-0134's open
   gap, #565). Only the footage skill listens.
9. **Split and merge** have no verb. They are full-element rewrites by the agent.
