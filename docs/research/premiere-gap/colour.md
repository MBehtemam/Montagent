### How Montagent handles colour at v0.2.0, in one paragraph

Colour exists only as **per-element `effects`**: four colour-filter scalars (`tint`, `saturation`,
`brightness`, `contrast`, ADR-0049), two matte operations (`mask`, `chroma`, ADR-0088/0152), and
`posterize`/`glow`/`grain` (ADR-0156). All are animatable (ADR-0146). There is no adjustment
layer, no shared operation across elements (`group` is a render-inert label, CONTEXT **Group**) and
no container (ADR-0166 neither admits nor refuses one). So a grade cannot be applied to a track, to
a set of elements or to the composited frame. **There is no colour management at all.** The
decoder asks `ffmpeg` for `-pix_fmt rgba` (8-bit) and accepts its default YUV→RGB conversion
(`decode.rs`). The painter does all arithmetic on stored sRGB-encoded values (ADR-0146 §2,
ADR-0147 §2). The encoder writes 8-bit `yuv420p` H.264 with no
`-colorspace`/`-color_primaries`/`-color_trc`/`-color_range` tags (`encode.rs`,
`Settings::args`), and ADR-0143 §5 exposes no encoder option. No tool reads luma or chroma back
out of a frame. `measure` reads keyed-alpha coverage (ADR-0088) and `verify` reads size, timing
and audio, but neither reads colour. An effect cannot be switched off without deleting it: video
effects have no `enabled` field (schema `/$defs/Effect`).

## Notable gaps

1. **No colour management at all, and the one fixed path is undeclared.** Decode goes through
   `ffmpeg`'s default YUV→RGBA, and the render is 8-bit `yuv420p` with **no colour tags**. No
   source colour space, data range or CICP tag is read. A player guesses the matrix, transfer and
   range, so a render can shift on playback. Of everything here, this is the most concrete defect
   and the cheapest to close: a fixed tag, with no new field.
2. **Log, raw and HDR footage cannot be handled.** No input transform, no tone mapping (three
   algorithms, input and output), no gamut compression and no above-8-bit path exist. The LUT route
   Premiere users take instead is refused (ADR-0040, ADR-0049). Log footage stays flat with no
   correct remedy, and HDR export (HDR10 metadata, PQ/HLG) is out of reach.
3. **A grade cannot be shared or applied above one element.** There is no adjustment layer, no
   sequence operation that runs on the composite, no custom group sharing operations by
   reference, and no nest. Every grade is copied onto every element. No ADR refuses any of these.
   Copied keyframes need re-timing because `t` is timeline milliseconds.
4. **No scopes and no colour reading.** An agent cannot measure exposure, clipping or a cast. It
   can only look at `frame` images. Color mode's HUDs and Video Scopes, the Lumetri scopes and the
   Video Limiter's gamut warning all fall here. A per-frame luma and chroma reading fits ADR-0024's
   "derivation, no verdict" shape for `measure`, and nothing refuses it.
5. **No tonal-range or hue-range operations.** Highlights, Shadows, the three wheels, split
   toning, Color mode's zones and its Sat/Hue/Lum Shift controls are all missing. Curves are
   refused, but the Shift controls and an S-curve contrast are fixed-arity parameter sets that
   ADR-0049's test does not rule out. The closed `tint.color` exception means a hue- or
   colour-typed parameter needs its own justification.
6. **No viewing aids on `frame`.** No effects bypass (Global FX Mute, graded against original),
   no solo, no channel view, no split comparison and no matte view. Each would be a read-only
   option on an existing tool.
7. **Secondary correction is half-built.** `chroma` gives a colour key, but it has no inverted key
   and cannot gate a correction, so you can correct everything **except** a colour and never
   **only** that colour. Masks gate only by duplicating the element, nothing tracks them, and
   union and subtract are missing (expansion and opacity are refused by ADR-0152).
8. **Curves, LUTs, channel mixing, duotone and CDL are refused by ADR-0049 and ADR-0040.**
   They make up most of Lumetri's power-grading surface (24 refused rows, with mask expansion and
   opacity under ADR-0152 and linear compositing under ADR-0147).
9. **Small, unrefused scalar members:** `gamma`, `sharpen` (deferred by ADR-0156), `vibrance`, a
   temperature axis (named admissible in ADR-0049), exposure as gain, a contrast pivot and an
   effect bypass flag.
